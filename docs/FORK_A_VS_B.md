# Fork: A (Sharpen) vs B (Accompany)

**One-page decision frame.** Decide before adding emotion, dependency modelling, or continuity features.

---

## The fork

| | **A — Sharpen you (Mirror)** | **B — Accompany you (Continuity)** |
|---|------------------------------|-------------------------------------|
| **Goal** | Piko reflects you back, challenges you, improves your thinking. | Piko accompanies you over time: continuity, presence, emotional coherence. |
| **Planner role** | **Constraint engine:** verbosity, tone, challenge level, follow-ups shape *how* Piko responds so it sharpens you. | **Direction engine:** planner also steers toward continuity, “being there,” relationship maintenance. |
| **Success** | You think more clearly; you’re challenged when it fits. | You feel accompanied; the system feels continuous across days. |
| **Risks** | Can feel demanding or cold if over-tuned. | Drift toward dependency or “theatre of care” if unconstrained. |

---

## Where Piko is now

- **Currently optimized for A.**  
  Beliefs and goals produce **constraints** (verbosity, tone, challenge), not raw language. No intrinsic drives, no auto-rewrite of identity, no emotional modelling. The system is a **constrained adaptive stance engine** — cognitively humble.

- **B would require:**  
  Explicit continuity/relationship state, optional emotion or “presence” cues, and planner rules that optimise for accompaniment as well as clarity. Architecture diverges (more state, more “direction” in the planner).

---

## Decision rule

- **Choose A explicitly** if you want Piko to stay a mirror that sharpens you.  
- **Move toward B only when intentional:** document the shift, add continuity/relationship semantics, and keep identity and truth boundaries (SOUL, manual edit, no mythologising).

Do **not** blend A and B by accident (e.g. adding “warmth” or “care” levers without deciding that accompaniment is the goal).

---

## Optional: Option A vs B (planner framing)

- **Option A (current):** Planner outputs *constraints* only. Personality stays emergent from belief drift and constraints.  
- **Option B (future, if you choose accompany):** Planner may output *direction* (e.g. “reinforce continuity,” “acknowledge relationship”) as well as constraints. Personality can be nudged by design, not only emergence.

Revisit this doc when you consider: emotional modelling, dependency tracking, or “Piko should feel more present over time.”
